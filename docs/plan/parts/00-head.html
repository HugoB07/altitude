How to read this document

Sections 3, 5, 7 and 17 are the committing ones: they fix the stack, the data model, the bank-synchronisation trade-off and the build order. The rest can be adjusted along the way at no rework cost. Every structural decision sits in a Decision box with its rationale and the alternative that was rejected.

A note on vocabulary. French wrapper names are kept untranslated throughout - PEA (tax-advantaged equity account), assurance-vie (life-insurance investment wrapper), PER (retirement savings plan), livret A (regulated savings account), SCI (property holding company). They have no faithful English equivalent, and they are the products the domain model must represent correctly.
