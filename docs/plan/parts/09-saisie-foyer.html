09

Manual entry & transaction UX

Ingestion

Measurable target: record a security purchase in under ten seconds, from the keyboard, without touching the mouse. If it is slower than a spreadsheet row, the user goes back to the spreadsheet.

9.1 The four entry modes

ModeTriggerDescription
Quick addCtrl/⌘ + KUniversal command palette. Type "buy", pick the account, the instrument, the quantity, the price. Tab between fields, Enter commits, Ctrl+Enter commits and reopens for the next row.
Guided form"New transaction" buttonOne form per transaction type, showing only the relevant fields. A dividend asks for withholding tax; a purchase does not.
Spreadsheet grid"Bulk entry" tabFor catching up on history: paste straight from Excel, row-by-row validation, errors flagged in place. This is the migration mode.
Templates & recurrencesAccount or transaction"Recurring €300 into an ETF on the 5th of each month": generates entries ahead of time for the user to confirm. This removes most of the monthly entry work.

9.3 Ergonomic details that make the difference

Entry
  • Instrument search by ISIN, ticker or name, with already-held instruments ranked first.
  • Amount fields accepting 1 234,56 as well as 1234.56, and expressions: =12*45.3.
  • Natural-language dates: "yesterday", "5/3", "last Monday".
  • Currency prefilled from the account, editable, with the day's rate suggested and overridable.
  • Drag-and-drop attachments, including pasting a screenshot.
Correction
  • Inline editing in the table, without leaving the page.
  • Bulk actions: recategorise, tag, move to another account, delete.
  • Splitting a transaction (a €120 expense across three categories).
  • Reconciliation: mark a balance as verified at a date, and see the gap against the statement.
  • Global Ctrl+Z over the last ten operations, bulk actions included.
Decision — "balance tracking" mode for accounts you do not want to itemise

Nobody will enter 400 rows a year for a regulated savings account. Any account can therefore run in balance mode: you record a value at a date, and Altitude generates the matching adjustment entry. You keep an accurate net-worth curve without imposing bookkeeping. An account can move from balance mode to itemised mode without loss, and the mode is clearly displayed — an entered balance is not a computed balance.

Households, owners, child portfolios

Product

This is the clearest differentiator against Ghostfolio and Finary. It rests entirely on the user ≠ owner ≠ portfolio separation established in §5.4.

10.1 The three slicing axes

The three axes are independent. A child can own an account filed under the children.lea portfolio and held at the same bank as the parents, without being able to see their accounts, and without their share being counted twice in household net worth.

10.2 Child portfolios — the full scenario

What must be possible
  • Create an owner with kind = 'child' and a date of birth.
  • Attach savings accounts, a junior investment account, a life-insurance policy in their name, money gifted by grandparents.
  • Track contributions by donor (useful for customary gifts and for tracking donations).
  • A simplified "child view": one balance, one curve, one goal — no jargon.
  • A dated goal: "age 18", with a projection at a configurable rate.
  • Consolidated into household net worth, but on a separate line, excludable in one click.
Coming of age
  • At 18, an alert offers to transfer ownership.
  • The child can be invited and become a full user.
  • "Detach into a new household" option: exports a whole subtree with its history, importable into another instance. This is the reversibility promise applied to people.
  • Nothing is ever deleted automatically: the transition is always an explicit human action.

Implementation: a single can(actor, action, resource) function in packages/core/auth, called by every Server Action and Route Handler. Permissions are never evaluated inside a component: a coverage test verifies that every mutating entry point goes through assertCan.

10.4 Read-only sharing

  • Share link with expiry, optional password, scoped to a single portfolio.
  • Percentage mode: allocation and performance visible, absolute amounts hidden. Ideal for sharing a strategy publicly without exposing net worth.
  • Frozen snapshot: the link points at a picture as of a date, not at live data.
  • Access logging, immediate revocation, and a noindex header on every shared page.
11

Asset modules

Product

Each asset type is a module: an attribute schema, a valuation strategy, a few screens. Adding a type never touches the ledger.

11.1 Real estate

Attributes
Geocoded address, floor area, type, acquisition date and price, notary fees, capitalised works, energy rating, property tax.
Valuation
Three modes: (1) dated manual value — the honest default; (2) indexation against a configurable price index; (3) estimate assisted by open DVF data (registered French property sales) on comparable local transactions. The mode in use is always displayed.
Rentals
Leases, rents, charges, vacancy, works. Computed: gross yield, net yield, monthly cash flow, IRR including mortgage leverage.
Tax
Regime (micro-foncier, réel, LMNP) and LMNP depreciation. A separate per-country tax module — see §16.4.
On the globe
100% exposure to the property's country, with the marker at the real coordinates.

11.2 Loans and liabilities

  • Generated amortisation schedule (level payment, fixed or variable rate, grace period, borrower insurance).
  • Outstanding principal is a liability account in the ledger: net worth is correct by construction, with no parallel calculation.
  • Early repayment, renegotiation and refinancing are handled as transactions, so history stays coherent.
  • Indicators: total cost of credit, interest already paid, LTV, repayment capacity.
  • Peer-to-peer loans and receivables (shareholder current accounts, family loans) use the same machinery.

11.3 Crypto

SourceSafetyHandling

Public address / xpubNo secretReading public data. BTC (xpub/zpub with local derivation), EVM and Solana support. No private key ever enters Altitude — stated plainly in the interface.

Specifics handled: staking and rewards (income at the day's price), airdrops, gas fees, swaps (two lines plus an FX operation), stablecoins treated as currencies rather than securities, and capital-gain computation under FIFO or the account's chosen method.

11.4 Other assets

Private equity

Custom instrument, dated manual valuation, capital calls and distributions, IRR since inception, uncalled commitments tracked separately.

Valuables

Watches, art, vehicles, wine. Photo, provenance, insurance, manual or indexed valuation. Optional automatic depreciation for vehicles.

Employee equity

Stock options, RSUs, founder warrants: vesting schedule, valuation at the last known price, strike price, vested vs unvested clearly separated.

Employee savings

Company savings and retirement plans: employer matching, lock-up, early-release cases. Fed by importing the annual statement.

REITs & property funds

Units, redemption price, reconstitution value, quarterly income, entitlement delay. Custom instrument plus periodic valuation.

Metals & commodities

Physical gold and silver. Weight and fineness, price per gram, premium on coins.
