Vision, prior art, positioning

Product

2.1 The problem Altitude solves

Finary proved there is strong demand for a unified view of net worth — banking, securities, crypto, property — rather than yet another budgeting tool. Its structural limits are the opening:

Observed limitConsequence for the userAltitude's answer
Data held by a third partyYour entire net worth, your IBANs and your positions transit through and reside with a vendor.Full self-hosting, database included. No outbound call beyond explicitly configured providers.
Tiered pricingAnalysis features (fine-grained allocation, performance) sit behind a subscription.Everything included, no cap on assets or accounts.
Fixed asset coverageNo way to model a property holding company, forestry shares, or a watch.Extensible account types plus schema-validated JSONB attributes, and asset plugins.
Single-userCouples handle joint ownership poorly; children's accounts fall outside the model entirely.Households, multiple owners, ownership shares, child portfolios (§10).
Limited exportLock-in: getting your data out is painful.Complete export (canonical JSON + per-table CSV) and import of the same format. Target: total reversibility.
Opaque calculationsYou cannot tell whether the displayed return is a TWR, an IRR or a raw change.The method is named, documented and tested against public references (§6.3).

2.2 Open-source prior art — what to borrow, what to avoid

No existing project covers net worth and multi-owner households and real estate. Each teaches something.

ProjectStack / licenceStrength worth borrowingGap that leaves room
GhostfolioAngular + NestJS + Prisma + Postgres + Redis, AGPLClosest competitor: securities and crypto tracking, polished dashboards, Docker self-hosting.Securities-centric: no double-entry, real estate and liabilities are marginal, no multi-owner household, limited import.
Firefly IIIPHP / Laravel, AGPLThe reference for consumer-grade double-entry: rules, budgets, recurrences, solid multi-currency, mature API.Budget and cash-flow oriented: no security positions, no market valuation, no allocation analysis.
Actual BudgetLocal-first JS, CRDT sync, MITLocal-first design and encrypted sync: excellent inspiration for privacy and offline behaviour.Envelope budgeting only, no net worth tracking.
Sure community forkRuby on Rails, AGPLCommunity fork of maybe-finance/maybe, archived in July 2025; covers budgeting and net worth, Docker self-hosting.Rails ecosystem (less accessible to the JS community targeted here); aggregation historically depended on a paid vendor.
KresusNode + woob, AGPLThe French precedent: self-hosted bank aggregation through scraping.Precisely demonstrates scraping's fragility: breaks on every bank redesign, 2FA friction. See §7.5.
Portfolio PerformanceJava desktop, GPLThe calculation reference: TWR, IRR, Modified Dietz, lot handling. Use it as a test oracle.Single-machine desktop application, steep learning curve, no web interface.
Beancount / hledgerPlain text, double-entryModel rigour: invariants, immutability, reversibility. Directly inspires the schema in §5.Technical users only, no consumer interface.
Decision — positioning

Altitude = Firefly III's accounting rigour + Ghostfolio's market valuation + Portfolio Performance's calculation accuracy, in a modern web interface, with two additions none of them offer: multi-owner households with child portfolios, and French wrappers (PEA, assurance-vie, PER) modelled correctly.

2.4 Guiding principles

PrincipleWhat it means concretely in the code
Your data is yoursComplete, re-importable export from day one. No computed field that cannot be reconstructed. Deleting an account genuinely purges.
Offline by defaultThe application is fully functional without internet access, except for quote refreshes. No font, script or icon loaded from a third-party CDN.
Accuracy before eleganceA wrong number is worse than no number. Every aggregate carries its method and its as-of date. Estimated values are visually distinct from observed ones.
The ledger is immutablePast entries are never edited: they are reversed. The audit log is append-only. This is what makes an import undoable.
Extend without forkingAsset types, import presets, price providers and connectors are declared interfaces, not switch statements in the core.
Manual entry is first-classIt is not the degraded mode of synchronisation: it is the reference mode. It must be faster than a spreadsheet row.
